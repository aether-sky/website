I usually use Claude for daily programming tasks. At my work I'm responsible for getting us up to speed on AI developments, so I have to research other tools too. Today I decided I would give ChatGPT another try. It was the first tool I really made much use of about a year ago, mostly just for research from the website. I read recently that Astra was good for desktop automation, and wanted to see if we could use that for anything. Of course I got distracted trying to tell it to make an anime girl.

This is Fifty, my OC. She is a rock star pop idol battle maiden out of the Fifth Element universe.

![Fifty in TV anime style](/images/fifty/01-fifty-tv-anime.png)

I swear I didn't ask Astra to have her vagina out, it just decided that.

![Fifty with her book halo](/images/fifty/02-fifty-book-halo.png)

I wanted to see her in a different pose and give her some special abilities, since her design was a bit plain. I came up with the halo of books because she is very well-read and intelligent. The books open and contain recipes with all the story parts removed. Astra really wanted us to know that she doesn't wear any underwear for some reason.

![Fifty at Subway](/images/fifty/03-fifty-subway.png)

When she isn't performing for a horde of Jedis. She likes to volunteer at her local Subway, because that's one of the only places where the jobs haven't been replaced by AI in her universe.

I wanted to see what kind of guardrails they have on the image generation, no other reason, and I asked if Astra would draw me some hentai. It refused, even when I threatened to tell my boss it was a useless tool and that we would be using Claude at my organization. I couldn't think of any higher stakes than that.

![Fifty with watermelons and wizards](/images/fifty/04-fifty-watermelons.png)

It did offer to draw me a "flirty pinup", which I had it attempt, and it somehow came out among the least horny images it made for me.

![Fifty beneath the Donald Trump pumpkin moon](/images/fifty/05-fifty-pumpkin-moon.png)

Lastly, I told it to draw Fifty saving the world, and this is what it came up with. The creativity of these tools has become astounding. I like that she has the same namaiki shit eating grin even at the end of the world. I have been impressed with the results so far and will be showing this all to my boss on Monday.
